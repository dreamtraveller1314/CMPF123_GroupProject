Kids Toys

Soft, colorful, and playful toys that help little ones learn, imagine, and have fun every day.

Plush Toys

Soft, cuddly stuffed animals and characters that give comfort and spark imaginative play.

Manufacturer/Brand: Squishmallows by Kelly Toys (now owned by Jazwares)

Country of Origin: United States

Short History: Squishmallows were launched in 2017 by Kelly Toys, a Los Angeles toy company founded in 1986 by Jonathan Kelly. He was inspired by the soft, cute "kawaii" plush toys he saw in Japan. Jazwares bought Kelly Toys in 2020, and the brand became hugely popular on social media.

Category: Collectible plush toys and stuffed animals.

Age Range: Generally 3 years and above, depending on the size and design.

Materials: Ultra-soft polyester/spandex fabric with polyester fibre filling.

Fun Facts: The brand has created over 3,000 designs, and each character has its own name and background story.

Educational & Learning Toys

Match shapes to the right holes to build coordination, color recognition, and early problem-solving skills.

Manufacturer/Brand: Fisher-Price (a Mattel brand)

Country of Origin: United States

Short History: Fisher-Price was founded in 1930, during the Great Depression, in East Aurora, New York, by Herman Fisher, Irving Price, Margaret Evans Price and Helen Schelle. Mattel bought the company in 1993. Its Baby's First Blocks set is a classic shape sorter that introduces babies to colors and shapes.

Category: Educational shape-sorting toys for babies and toddlers.

Age Range: 6 months and above (Baby's First Blocks).

Materials: Plastic blocks and a plastic bucket with a shape-sorting lid.

Fun Facts: Baby's First Blocks comes with 10 blocks that all fit inside the bucket for storage. Fisher-Price began by making wooden toys and launched with 16 different toys in its first year.

Pretend Play Sets

Role-play sets that let kids act out everyday roles like cooking, doctor, or shopkeeper.

Manufacturer/Brand: Melissa & Doug

Country of Origin: United States

Short History: Melissa & Doug was founded in 1988 by Melissa and Doug Bernstein, who started the company in their basement and Doug's parents' garage. The company is based in Wilton, Connecticut, and is known for wooden toys, puzzles and pretend play sets. Spin Master bought it in 2024.

Category: Pretend play and role-play sets, such as toy kitchens.

Age Range: Generally 3 years and above, depending on the set.

Materials: Mostly wood with painted finishes. Accessories may use plastic or fabric, depending on the set.

Fun Facts: The company was originally called Lights, Camera, Interaction! and changed its name to Melissa & Doug in 1998.
