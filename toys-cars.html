Hot Wheels vs. Matchbox

1. Hot Wheels

  • Brand: Mattel (USA)
  • History: Launched in 1968 with 16 cars to rival Matchbox.
  • Category: Die-cast cars (1:64)
  • Age: 3+
  • Materials: Metal body, plastic wheels
  • Fun fact: The pink Beach Bomb is one of the rarest toy cars.

2. Matchbox

  • Brand: Lesney (UK), now Mattel
  • History: Started in 1953 when Jack Odell made a tiny road roller for his daughter.
  • Category: Die-cast realistic vehicles
  • Age: 3+
  • Materials: Metal body, plastic parts
  • Fun fact: Its name comes from the matchbox size toys her school allowed.
