Detailed information and media for our toy collection.

Hot Wheels

Manufacturer/Brand: Mattel (USA)

Short History: Launched in 1968 with 16 cars ("The Original Sweet 16"), designed to be faster and flashier than rivals.

Category: Die-cast toy cars (1:64 scale).

Age Range: 3+ years old.

Materials: Metal body, plastic base and wheels

Fun Facts: A 1969 pink Beach Bomb prototype is one of the rarest toy cars in the world. Mattel also makes orange track sets for racing.

Matchbox

Manufacturer/Brand: Lesney Products (UK), now Mattel

Short History: Started in 1953. Jack Odell made a tiny road roller for his daughter because her school only allowed toys that fit in a matchbox.

Category: Die-cast realistic vehicles.

Age Range: 3+ years old.

Materials: Metal body, plastic parts

Fun fact: The packaging was literally a matchbox. Mattel bought the brand in 1997.

Tomica

Manufacturer/Brand: Tomy (Japan).

Short History: Launched in 1970 to showcase Japanese cars.

Category: Die-cast vehicles, often with opening doors and suspension

Age range: 3 to 7 years old.

Materials: Die-cast metal, plastic

Fun facts: Very popular in Japan, with models of buses, police cars and fire trucks.
