Puzzle

Brain teasers, jigsaw sets, and problem-solving toys that build focus, spatial thinking, and creativity for players of all ages.

Jigsaw Puzzles

Popular Brand: Ravensburger

Country of Origin: Germany

Short History: Originated in the 1760s when mapmaker John Spilsbury mounted maps on wood and cut along borders as a geography teaching aid, evolving into everyday picture jigsaws.

Category: Jigsaw & Picture Puzzles

Age range: 6+ years (varies by piece count)

Materials: Sturdy recycled cardboard / thick millboard with printed paper finish

Fun facts: Interlocking puzzle pieces only became standard in the early 20th century, helping pieces stay connected while assembling large scenes.

Mechanical & Twisting Puzzles

Popular Brand: Rubik's

Country of Origin: Hungary

Short History: Invented in 1974 by architecture professor Erno Rubik as a teaching tool for understanding 3D geometry and movement, becoming a worldwide puzzle icon in the 1980s.

Category: Mechanical & Twisting Puzzles

Age range: 8+ years

Materials: ABS plastic tiles and internal pivot core/mechanism

Fun facts: A standard 3x3 cube has over 43 quintillion possible scrambled states, yet any state can be solved in 20 moves or fewer ("God's Number").

Crossword, Word & Trivia Puzzles

Popular Brand: Mattel

Country of Origin: United States

Short History: Word puzzles grew popular in newspapers in the early 1900s, and letter-tile word board games expanded the format in the 1930s–1940s for group and family play.

Category: Crossword, Word & Trivia Puzzles

Age range: 8+ years

Materials: Cardboard or wood game board, letter tiles (plastic/wood), and tile racks

Fun facts: Letter distributions and point values in classic word games are balanced around how often letters appear in everyday vocabulary.
